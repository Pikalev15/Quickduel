# Timing pass (development only)

First pass, not final balance. One-board games intentionally stay short. Reaction uses five 1.5–3.5s waits, 1.25s tap windows and median score; false starts/misses receive the full 1.25s penalty. Easy Memory Grid shows the pattern for 2.5s with 7s answer; XL shows it for 4s with 10s answer. Odd One Out is unchanged at 6s. Typing Sprint uses 30s, ready keyboard before countdown, fixed-duration scoring. Cryptic practice is 40s. Frequency practice targets 71s with five complete audio playbacks, 8.2s per answer and 2s feedback; timeouts score zero and advance. Ranked settings depend on the separately staged SQL. No real device evidence yet.

Recruitment plan (do not contact testers yet): 5–10 first-time testers, mostly phones; include at least two iPhones and two Android devices if available. Test unassisted: Memory Grid, Odd One Out, Frequency, Typing, Cryptic; then Reaction. Record device/browser, whether at least one answer was submitted per non-reflex game, timeouts, misunderstandings, audio/keyboard failures and perceived difficulty. Aim for at least 90% submission attempts, not 90% correct answers. Record denominators (e.g. 9/10). Verify keyboard visually on physical iPhone Safari through countdown and delayed snapshot, including first keystroke, orientation change and refocus. Desktop/mobile emulation verifies focus/DOM only. Do not count mocked APIs as real multiplayer evidence. Do not merge or deploy production until candidate SQL is validated on a disposable database, both-player timing and cleanup are tested and a preview is approved.
